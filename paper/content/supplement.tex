\section{Article-type sensitivities (P13, P16 and P18)}\label{sec:s-articles}
The union-link filter is recorded in \texttt{POSTHOC.md} before the run. Publication types are unioned over the same
PMID/DOI links used to build the study populations. Journal Article is required; any linked review,
systematic review, meta-analysis, editorial, letter, comment, erratum, retraction-notice or expression-of-concern
type excludes the paper. Retracted Publication is retained because it labels the original article, not its notice.
Missing publication types are excluded and counted.
Neither unfiltered primary retracted/control group has missing types. For CD$_5$, 3,528 of 4,587 primary papers
and 43,152 of 52,147 controls satisfy the filter; all 3,528 retracted papers and 43,143 controls have defined
scores. Of these, 3,521 and 23,839, respectively, enter 1,841 comparison strata.
For CD$_3$, 5,600 of 7,350 primary papers and 56,842 of 68,190 controls satisfy the filter; 5,599 and 56,822 have
defined scores, and 5,592 and 37,334 enter 2,735 comparison strata. No controls are redrawn; thresholds remain those
of the reference sample. Thus this check isolates type eligibility, not an article-only recalibration
of the top-5\% threshold.

P16 finds DOI-only notice records targeting native PMIDs for 106 primary papers and 13 controls.
PMID priority removes propagation of any DOI-only excluded type, not just notices.
It retains 3,626 primary papers and 43,167 drawn controls, recovering 98 and 15, respectively.
CD$_5$ compares 3,619 with 24,192 in 1,898 strata; CD$_3$ retains 5,745 and 56,861, comparing 5,736 with 37,776
in 2,808 strata. Table~2 of the main text uses PMID priority; Table~\ref{tab:articles} uses union links.
The PMID-prioritized DI$_{nok}$ gaps are 0.073 (0.035--0.109; 3,569 compared papers) and
0.083 (0.054--0.108; 5,555), respectively, using whole-journal intervals.

For the union-link type definition, the CD$_5$ conditional-logit positive-score odds ratios are 1.25 (1.13--1.37) unadjusted,
1.33 (1.21--1.47) with log references, and 1.34 (1.22--1.48) with log references and citations
(2,740 retracted papers in 1,165 outcome-varying strata). The corresponding top-5\% odds ratios are
1.07 (0.86--1.34), 1.64 (1.28--2.10) and 1.39 (1.08--1.80)
(1,524 retracted papers in 383 outcome-varying strata).
The matched mean-log reference difference is $+0.064$ (0.045--0.083; group means 3.626 and 3.562).
The matched mean-log citation difference is $+0.265$ (0.233--0.299).
The union-link DI$_{nok}$ mean gap is 0.074 (0.035--0.107; 3,471 compared papers) for CD$_5$ and
0.082 (0.053--0.107; 5,413) for CD$_3$, with whole-journal intervals from P16.

P18 counts the publication types behind PMID-priority failures (overlapping counts; a paper can have several).
Among 961 failing primary papers: review types 395, editorial 10, letter 48, comment 15, published erratum 16,
retraction of publication 458, expression of concern 47, and no Journal Article type 63; 682 also carry Retracted
Publication. Assigned once in the order review; letter, comment or editorial; erratum, retraction-notice or
expression-of-concern types; no Journal Article, they split 395, 67, 499 and 0. Among 8,980 failing drawn
controls: review types 6,032, editorial 631, letter 1,361, comment 1,663, published erratum 228, retraction of
publication 37, expression of concern 6, and no Journal Article 2,305; the ordered split is 6,032, 2,518, 265
and 165. No paper in either group lacks type metadata.

\section{Topic matching (P16)}\label{sec:s-topic}
For each retracted paper, up to five original controls from its journal and year are selected by the greatest
positive Jaccard overlap of descriptor-level major MeSH tags. A fixed hash breaks ties, and a control can be used
more than once. Each paper is compared with the average score of its selected controls and, as a baseline, with all
controls in its stratum that have usable tags. Both comparisons are repeated with the article-type filter, and the
matches stay fixed during whole-journal resampling.

The frozen metadata include major-topic tags attached to descriptors, but not those attached only to qualifiers.
Thus the check does not cover all major MeSH headings.

Of 4,518 primary papers with defined unfiltered DI$_{nok}$, 1,722 have these tags and 540 find overlapping
controls, across 140 journals. The other 1,182 have no eligible overlap.
The article-type version retains 417 papers in 112 journals; 938 of 1,355 papers with usable tags have no match.
Both intervals include zero (Table~3 of the main text), so neither version establishes a gap.
Each baseline compares exactly the same retained papers with all controls in their journal/year that have usable
tags. Comparing these baselines with the topic-matched estimates separates the choice of controls from the loss
of papers without a match.
