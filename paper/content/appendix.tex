\section{Exploratory diagnostics and accounting}\label{sec:appendix}
The identifiers P1--P16 label one set of exploratory analyses, indexed in \texttt{POSTHOC.md}; the experiment index and
claim-to-run map link them to immutable results. These analyses were added after the primary results were known.

\subsection{Reference and citation counts (P1)}
The primary-set retracted papers have overall median reference counts of 40, against 35 for the full control pool,
and median window citations of 19 against 13. In the reference sample, CD$_5$ decreases with reference count
(Spearman $\rho=-0.19$) and with window
citations ($\rho=-0.43$). The lower reference-count quartile is 29 for primary-set retracted papers and 22 for
the full control pool.

\subsection{Margins and interval interpretation (P3)}
P3 evaluates conventional margins with two one-sided tests
\citep{schuirmann1987tost,lakens2017equivalence}: $\pm0.1$ reference-sample standard deviations for mean differences
and 0.80--1.25 for odds ratios. The resulting mean margin, $\pm0.0044$, exceeds the observed review effect
($-0.0033$); the score SD is tail-sensitive, with a scored-set 5th--95th percentile span of about 0.028.
The top-5\% odds ratio's 90\% interval is
0.85--1.16.

\subsection{Notice records (P8)}
In the CD$_5$ window, 36.7\% of papers retracted inside the window have a citing record identified by PubMed as a
retraction notice, erratum or expression of concern, against 4.7\% of the full control pool; so do 18.4\% of papers
retracted after the window. Removing notice records lowers the positive-score odds ratio from 1.71 (1.60--1.82;
5,858 compared papers) to 1.44 (1.34--1.54; 5,857 compared papers) for papers retracted inside the window,
and from 1.50 to 1.35 (1.28--1.42) for all retracted papers. There are 5,861 and 5,860 scored papers, respectively;
the compared counts exclude strata without a scored control. The primary-set estimate changes little,
from 1.23 to 1.24 (1.14--1.34).

\subsection{Placebo timing and annual scores (P9 and P12)}
Among papers retracted at least four years after publication, a placebo notice three years before the real one
gives a difference in differences in DI$_{nok}$ of 0.036 (0.012--0.060; 2,565 compared papers), against
0.064 (0.037--0.093; 2,763 compared papers) for the real notice. By reason, the placebo change is close to zero
for honest error (0.005, $-0.065$ to 0.077; real notice 0.079, 0.012--0.149) but not for misconduct
(0.042, 0.010--0.074; real notice 0.078, 0.041--0.115).

Figure~\ref{fig:event} uses the balanced population with annual calendar coverage from three years before to three
years after the notice. P12 estimates the mean paired annual gap and its 95\% strata-bootstrap interval.
At event years $-3,-2,-1,0,1,2,3$, respectively, the numbers of defined pairs are
2,220, 2,504, 2,468, 2,440, 2,225, 1,982 and 1,854. Missing annual DI$_{nok}$ scores are not replaced by zero.
Mean control citations in this population range from 4.0 to 4.9 per year; it is distinct from the 5,100-paper
three-year-window comparison.

\subsection{Denominator size (P10)}
The $n_k$ term accounts for at least 90\% of the CD$_5$ denominator for 97.9\% of papers.
Median counts are reported in the main citation-composition results.

\subsection{Three-year window and control accounting (P2 and P11)}
The 52,147 controls were drawn for all 10,450 retracted papers. The CD$_5$ primary comparison uses 2,239 strata
with 4,586 scored retracted papers and 31,674 controls, of which 12 have undefined scores, leaving 31,662.
The remaining controls belong to strata without a primary retracted paper in the comparison.
Pooled shares in the used strata are 18.1\% positive (5,725/31,662), 3.3\% above the top-5\% threshold
(1,047/31,662), and 0.56\% above the top-1\% threshold (176/31,662).
Weighting each stratum by its compared retracted papers instead gives 17.3\%, 3.1\% and 0.54\%, the shares
reported in the main comparisons. CD$_3$ has 7,349 scored primary papers, of which 7,348 are compared with
48,044 scored controls in 3,308 strata.

\subsection{Article-type sensitivities (P13 and P16)}
The union-link filter is recorded in \texttt{POSTHOC.md} before the run. Publication types are unioned over the same
PMID/DOI links used to build the study populations. Journal Article is required; any linked review,
systematic review, meta-analysis, editorial, letter, comment, erratum, retraction-notice or expression-of-concern
type excludes the paper. Retracted Publication is retained because it labels the original article, not its notice.
Missing publication types are excluded and counted.
Neither unfiltered primary retracted/control group has missing types. For CD$_5$, 3,528 of 4,587 primary papers
and 43,152 of 52,147 controls satisfy the filter; all 3,528 retracted papers and 43,143 controls have defined
scores. Of these, 3,521 and 23,839, respectively, enter 1,841 comparison strata.
For CD$_3$, 5,600 of 7,350 primary papers and 56,842 of 68,190 controls satisfy the filter; 5,599 and 56,822 have
defined scores, and 5,592 and 37,334 enter 2,735 comparison strata. No controls are redrawn; thresholds remain those
of the reference sample. Thus this check isolates type eligibility, not an article-only recalibration
of the top-5\% threshold.

P16 finds DOI-only notice records targeting native PMIDs for 106 primary papers and 13 controls.
PMID priority removes propagation of any DOI-only excluded type, not just notices.
It retains 3,626 primary papers and 43,167 drawn controls, recovering 98 and 15, respectively.
CD$_5$ compares 3,619 with 24,192 in 1,898 strata; CD$_3$ retains 5,745 and 56,861, comparing 5,736 with 37,776
in 2,808 strata. Table~\ref{tab:rob} uses PMID priority; Table~\ref{tab:articles} uses union links.
The PMID-prioritized DI$_{nok}$ gaps are 0.073 (0.035--0.109; 3,569 compared papers) and
0.083 (0.054--0.108; 5,555), respectively, using whole-journal intervals.

\subsection{Author-name sensitivity (P14)}
P14 was planned in \texttt{POSTHOC.md} before execution. Across the full frozen snapshot,
1,649 normalized names occur on at least ten distinct non-reinstated retracted paper keys; 22,028 of 62,734 such
keys carry at least one of those names. Duplicate notices are unioned by DOI, or PMID when DOI is absent.
Names are split on semicolons, trimmed, whitespace-collapsed and case-normalized, without initials merging or
fuzzy matching. Blank fields and exact placeholders (unknown, [unknown], n/a, not available) count as missing.
Any linked qualifying key excludes a primary node; controls are not author-screened or redrawn.
All 4,587 CD$_5$ and 7,350 CD$_3$ primary nodes have names on at least one linked non-reinstated key.

The rule removes 2,280 CD$_5$ primary papers, leaving 2,307 scored and 2,306 compared with 23,999 controls in
1,363 strata. It removes 3,722 CD$_3$ primary papers, leaving 3,628 eligible, 3,627 scored and 3,626 compared
with 37,294 controls in 2,115 strata. Original scores and yearly thresholds remain fixed.
For CD$_5$, the positive-score odds ratio is 1.26 (1.12--1.41) and the top-5\% odds ratio 0.97 (0.75--1.25).
The CD$_3$ mean gap is 0.00063 ($-0.00047$ to $0.00184$), positive-score odds ratio 1.32 (1.21--1.44), and
top-5\% odds ratio 1.03 (0.85--1.25). Table~\ref{tab:authors} gives mean contrasts by reason class;
the repository gives the remaining odds ratios and coverage details.

\subsection{Citing-record exclusions and journal dependence (P15)}
The exclusions use the notice, publication-type and retraction flags described in Methods.
Across both publication cohorts and scoring windows, PubMed metadata are available for 1,384,201 of
1,700,837 citing events; 1,384,200 have a known publication type. The combined filter removes 37,961 events,
counting each once even when more than one exclusion applies.

For the five-year population, notice removal affects 844 of 4,587 primary papers and removes 1,228 citing records;
it affects 2,456 of the 52,147 drawn controls and removes 3,026 citing records. The full filter affects 2,400 primary
papers and 12,009 controls, removing 6,525 and 23,978 citing records, respectively.
It makes 13 primary and 116 control DI$_{nok}$ scores undefined. Table~\ref{tab:citercounts} distinguishes
defined from compared counts; controls are not redrawn.

Using original rather than remaining citation counts leaves a similar adjusted estimate:
0.09880 (0.06485--0.12920). The ten excluded journals contain 1,493 eligible five-year and 2,058 three-year
primary papers. Journals are ranked before filtering or checking whether a score remains defined.

The repository also reports the filtered reason-class estimates; the main comparison and Table~\ref{tab:citers}
focus on the overall contrast.

\subsection{Topic matching and uncertainty (P16)}
The frozen metadata include major-topic tags attached to descriptors, but not those attached only to qualifiers.
Thus the check does not cover all major MeSH headings.

Of 4,518 primary papers with defined unfiltered DI$_{nok}$, 1,722 have these tags and 540 find overlapping
controls, across 140 journals. The other 1,182 have no eligible overlap.
The article-type version retains 417 papers in 112 journals; 938 of 1,355 papers with usable tags have no match.
Both intervals include zero (Table~\ref{tab:citers}), so neither version establishes a gap.
Each baseline compares exactly the same retained papers with all controls in their journal/year that have usable
tags. Comparing these baselines with the topic-matched estimates separates the choice of controls from the loss
of papers without a match.

All fixed-window DI$_{nok}$ mean intervals resample whole journals, keeping their publication years together.
The planned CD comparisons and annual event-time estimates instead retain journal--year resampling.
Detailed interval-source mappings and validation checks are in the repository.
