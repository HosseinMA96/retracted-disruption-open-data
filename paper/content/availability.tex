Citation data \citep{arjomandi2024dataset}, OpenCitations metadata \citep{ocmeta2024dump} and linked Retraction Watch
labels \citep{arjomandi2026retracteddata} are public; PubMed metadata use the 2026 baseline from the
U.S. National Library of Medicine.
Scripts, specifications, checksummed records, aggregate tables and the claim-to-result map are available at
\ifanon [repository link removed for anonymized review]\else
\url{https://github.com/HosseinMA96/retracted-disruption-open-data}\fi. Per-paper scores of non-retracted papers are
not released because they could be mistaken for integrity labels, not because of a source-data restriction.
The public inputs and code allow recomputation. The version described here is tagged as release \texttt{v1.0.1}.

\paragraph{Pre-specification record} The design was recorded on 10 November 2026 (UTC) before scoring;
the unchanged specification, hashes and detailed timing records are documented in
\texttt{SPEC.md} and \texttt{spec\_provenance.json}.
The timestamps come from local clocks and private history, so these records are not an independently
verifiable public preregistration.
